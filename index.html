Questions we're often asked

How long does gel polish last?+

Usually two to three weeks, depending on how fast your nails grow and how you use your hands. Wearing gloves for cleaning helps it last longer.

Do I need to book in advance?+

Booking is best so we can keep time just for you. If we have a free slot, walk-ins are welcome too. Message us on WhatsApp to check.

Can I bring my own design idea?+

Yes, please do! Share a photo or describe the look, and we'll suggest what works best for your nail shape and length before we start.

Will extensions damage my natural nails?+

Not when they're applied and removed properly. We prep gently and never rip or pry off product. Please come to us for removal rather than peeling them off.

Do you offer bridal trials?+

Yes. We recommend a trial one to two weeks before the wedding, so we can match your outfit and jewellery and perfect the design.

How can I pay?+

We accept UPI and cash.
